## Brand

- Primary: `#3B2F8F`
- Accent: `#FF8A00`
- Teal: `#14B8A6`
- Page: `#FAFAFC`

Tokens live in `packages/core/src/tokens.css` and are exposed by Tailwind v4's `@theme static` directive.

Use `bg-sg-primary`, `text-sg-muted`, `rounded-sg`, and related utilities in a consuming app that compiles Tailwind.
